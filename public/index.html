    📖 How to Play

    Goal: Be the richest player on the Road to Riches. Your pawn moves as your net worth grows — reach the crown at the end of the 40-tile road.

    • Careers: Pick a career ladder, then work shifts to earn that level's salary. Promotions need 5 shifts at your level AND playtime (60s for the first promotion, 180s for the second).
    • Shift cap: You can work 3 shifts every 24 hours — no unlimited grinding. Plan your shifts.
    • Homes: Buy homes to collect daily rent as they grow in value. Every 30 days each home charges utilities (2% of its value).
    • Cars: Buy scarce cars to boost your net worth. Some cars are limited — when they're gone, they're gone.
    • Marketplace: List homes for sale to other players, and buy homes they list.
    • Board tiles: Special tiles grant rewards — cash bonuses, a tax return, and an exclusive car you can't buy anywhere else.
    • Wallet: Tap Wallet anytime for a quickview of your cash, homes, cars, net worth, and next utility charge.
    • Points: Earn PulseVR Points for playtime and milestones.
    • Messages: Message other players privately from the Mail section. A badge pings you when new mail arrives.
