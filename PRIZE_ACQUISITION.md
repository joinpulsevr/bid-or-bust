## 5-minute prep (do once before 10/21)

1. **Meta account:** make sure your Meta account has a working payment method
   (Horizon mobile app → Menu → Wallet). The Quest app gifts charge to it.
2. **VRChat account:** make sure the account you'll host from can complete a
   purchase (VRC+ gifting bills $9.99 at gift time).
3. **Cash App:** $joinpulsevr funded / linked, ready to send $5 + $20 + $25.

## How to gift a Meta Quest app ($5 and $10 lots)

Direct app gifting is built into the Meta Horizon Store — no gift cards needed.

1. Open the **Meta Horizon mobile app** (or the Horizon Store website / in-headset store).
2. Search for the app you want to gift (pick any app at ~$5 / ~$10 — decide the
   titles ahead of time or let the winner choose on the night).
3. Tap the **gift box icon** at the top of the app's page.
4. Enter the winner's name or **email address** (their Meta account email works best).
5. Add an optional gift note, tap **Confirm** — done, charged to your Meta payment method.

The winner gets an email with a 25-digit code; they redeem it on the app's
purchase page (Options → Redeem gift). One catch, verified with Meta's help docs:
**app subscriptions can't be gifted** — only one-time app purchases. The $5/$10
lots are one-time apps, so you're fine.

*Fallback:* if a winner can't receive a direct gift, a Meta Quest gift card works,
but third-party cards start at $15 — direct gifting is cleaner for exact amounts.

Sources: Meta help — "Send and receive app gifts from the Meta Horizon Store"
(https://www.meta.com/help/quest/291343079251233/);
Meta help — app subscriptions can't be gifted
(https://www.meta.com/en-gb/help/quest/282979763452445/)

## How to gift VRChat Plus ($10 lot)

Gift it straight from the winner's VRChat profile — no need to be in the same instance.

1. In VRChat, open the **Search** or **Social** tab and find the winner's profile.
2. Click **Gift VRChat+** (labeled "Gift VRC+" in some clients).
3. Complete the $9.99 purchase.

Notes, verified with the VRChat wiki:
- If the winner **already has VRC+**, the gift extends their existing subscription —
  still a clean $10-equivalent prize, no waste.
- You can also gift from in-instance (click the player → Gift VRChat+), and there
  are bulk "gift drop" bundles with discounts — irrelevant here since you only need one.

Source: VRChat Wiki — VRC+ gifting (https://wiki.vrchat.com/wiki/Special:MyLanguage/VRChat+)

## On the night — fulfillment order

After each lot's reveal, the host (you) fulfills before moving on:
prize lots → gift/send immediately; bust lots → nothing to send (the penalty is
in-game). Keep the show moving: Quest/VRC+ gifts take ~1 minute each; have the
winner's Meta email / VRChat username ready by asking in chat as lots are won.
